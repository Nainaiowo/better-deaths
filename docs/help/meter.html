Damage Meter

Follow damage without leaving Better Deaths

The Widgets tab places the Death Widget on the left and DPS Meter on the right. The Meter records combat damage inside duties and saves completed encounters in its own browser.

Encounter browser

Live encounter follows active combat. Completed damage encounters appear beneath it, while Example provides a redacted report preview outside combat.

Party and All

Party is the normal ranking and includes party members, their recognized pets, the party limit break, and unattributed outgoing damage. All also shows enemies and other combatants.

Ability details

Use the arrow beside a combatant to expand their actions. Each action shows damage, DPS, share, critical-hit rate, and direct-hit rate.

How the numbers are handled

WidgetOpens a resizable meter that shows the current encounter and falls back to the latest completed encounter while idle.
PreviewUses the same renderer as the separate widget. It remains available outside combat and updates immediately as columns or display mode change.
ColumnsDrag active columns to reorder them and select X to remove one. Select + on an available column, click its tile, or drag it into the active row to add it.
DisplayNormal keeps full labels and player names. Concise uses initials, shorter headers, and tighter column widths.
Clear historyCtrl+click the trash icon in the encounter browser. Damage encounter history is stored separately from Review pulls.
DamageUses the amount reported by the game after mitigation, blocking, parrying, and other reductions.
DPSYour personal damage divided by the time from the first recorded combat event through the latest party damage event.
rDPSYour raid contribution. Damage gained from another player's raid buffs is moved back to the player who provided those buffs. Your personal DPS remains available beside it.
PetsDamage from a recognized pet or companion is included under its owner while the pet's individual actions remain visible in the breakdown.
Limit breakParty limit break damage appears as its own source instead of being assigned to the player who pressed it.
Misses and immunityMissed, resisted, blocked, parried, and invulnerable results are retained. Only actual damage increases the total.
Why an estimated split may appear

The game reports ordinary damage-over-time effects as one combined tick. Better Deaths keeps that combined encounter damage exact, then uses the active effects and previously observed solo ticks to estimate how much belongs to each source. If no reliable owner exists, the damage remains visible as unattributed instead of being assigned to the wrong player.

How raid-buff credit is calculated

Percentage buffs use the statuses captured on each hit. Critical-hit and direct-hit buffs also use the player's observed unbuffed rates, so their live value may settle as more attacks are recorded. Moving this credit changes each player's rDPS, but it does not change the party's total damage.
